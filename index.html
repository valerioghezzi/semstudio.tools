How these tools work

Every tool is a single page that runs inside your browser. Output files stay on your computer: nothing is uploaded, no cookies are set, and semstudio.tools itself uses no analytics or tracking scripts. Each result comes with the formula behind it and the reference it comes from, and every interpretive cutoff is reported as guidance rather than as a rule.
